## Data

The extension contains no data of its own. Every figure it shows is derived
from public open data published by Spanish public bodies, and is reused under
the terms each publisher sets (in Spain, generally Ley 37/2007 on the reuse of
public-sector information, which allows reuse with attribution):

| Publisher | Data used | Terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ajuntament de Barcelona — [Open Data BCN](https://opendata-ajuntament.barcelona.cat) | IRIS citizen requests, Guàrdia Urbana incidents, population, administrative units | CC BY 4.0 |
| Generalitat de Catalunya — [Dades obertes](https://analisi.transparenciacatalunya.cat) | Mossos d'Esquadra known offences | the portal's open-data licence |
| Ayuntamiento de Madrid — [Datos abiertos](https://datos.madrid.es), [Geoportal](https://geoportal.madrid.es) | Avisos ciudadanos, Policía Municipal incidents, barrio boundaries | the portal's terms of use |
| [Instituto Nacional de Estadística (INE)](https://www.ine.es) | Household income atlas (ADRH), census-section maps | INE legal notice: reuse with attribution |
| [Ministerio del Interior](https://estadisticasdecriminalidad.ses.mir.es) | Balance de Criminalidad | reuse of public-sector information |
| [OpenStreetMap Nominatim](https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org) | Address lookup, only when the user sets an address | © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL |

The exact files, with links and download dates, are listed in each data
release's `meta.json` and in the extension's **Sources** window. The
publishers do not endorse this extension, and the derived scores are not
official figures.
