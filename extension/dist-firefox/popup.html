Street Quality Spain

Neighbourhood data on Idealista: Barcelona and Madrid street by street, the rest of Spain by municipality and census section

My address

Shows the straight-line distance on each listing and adds “Nearest to my address” to Idealista's “Ordenar” menu. It reorders the listings on the page you are on (Idealista shows 30 per page). The address is looked up once with OpenStreetMap Nominatim; type coordinates (“41.39, 2.17”) to skip that.

Layers and weights

Legend

60–100 good 40–59 average 0–39 poor

Score = 100 − weighted mean of the percentiles of the enabled layers that exist at the place: city-wide in Barcelona and in Madrid ("Street" badges), Spain-wide elsewhere ("Area" badges). The scales are not comparable with each other. Map hexes use the same score. "≈" = advertiser hid the address; "approx" = barri average.
